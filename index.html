SIDEWAYS

A new gravel stage every day. Five sectors, one clock, unlimited attempts.

How to drift
  • Tap brake while turning to kick the rear out.
  • Hold throttle to keep the drift going.
  • Lift off to rotate more, counter-steer to straighten or flick.
  • Space handbrake for hairpins.
  • G turns on the racing line assist, a gentle pull toward the ideal line.
  • R restarts the stage, T puts you back at the last checkpoint (+5 s).
